With the foundations in place, we now apply first principles to real-world system design problems. This part walks through high-level designs for common interview scenarios, such as URL shorteners and notification services, emphasizing scalability, availability, and efficiency.

\begin{designbox}
Focus on end-to-end flows: Start with requirements, sketch architectures, identify bottlenecks, and discuss optimizations. This structured approach mirrors top tech interviews at companies like Google or Amazon.
\end{designbox}

Drawing from resources like Kleppmann's work on data-intensive applications \cite{kleppmann2017}, we'll break down each system into components, trade-offs, and failure modes. Prepare to think about massive scale—handling billions of requests while keeping latency low.
